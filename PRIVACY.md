# 📦 Delicate — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Delicate Discord bot may collect and store, why that information is used, and how it is handled.

Delicate is a Discord moderation bot designed to help server staff manage moderation actions, warnings, and moderation history.

## 1. Information We Collect

When Delicate is used in a Discord server, it may collect and store information associated with moderation activity, including:

* Discord user ID
* Discord username
* Discord server ID
* Moderation action history
* Warning records
* Moderation reasons
* Moderation durations
* Case and warning numbers
* Dates and times of moderation actions
* Discord user ID and username of the staff member who performed a moderation action

Delicate does **not** intentionally collect:

* Passwords
* Payment information
* Discord account authentication credentials
* Private Discord messages

The bot only receives information made available to it through Discord and the permissions granted to it.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information collected by Delicate is used for Discord server administration and moderation purposes.

This may include:

* Managing moderation actions
* Maintaining warning history
* Applying automatic warning escalation
* Maintaining staff moderation logs
* Reviewing a member's moderation history
* Preventing abuse of the server
* Investigating moderation-related incidents
* Maintaining records of previous moderation actions

Delicate does not use moderation records for advertising or targeted marketing.

## 3. Where Information Is Stored

Moderation records are stored in a SQLite database on the system where the Delicate bot is hosted.

The database may contain moderation information associated with users and staff members.

The database is intended to remain private and should not be publicly accessible.

The person or organization operating a particular Delicate instance is responsible for securing its database and hosting environment.

## 4. Who Can Access the Information

Moderation records are intended to be accessible only to authorized staff members of the Discord server where Delicate is deployed.

The bot operator and authorized server administrators may have access to the database containing moderation records.

Information may also be processed by Discord as part of providing the Discord platform and its services.

We do not intentionally make moderation records publicly available.

## 5. Data Retention

Moderation records may be retained for as long as they are reasonably useful for:

* Maintaining server safety
* Resolving moderation disputes
* Identifying repeated rule violations
* Maintaining an accurate moderation history

Retention practices may vary depending on how the server administrator operates Delicate.

Server administration may remove or modify records when appropriate.

## 6. Data Sharing

The bot operator does not intentionally sell, rent, or trade information collected by Delicate.

Moderation information is not intentionally shared with third parties except where reasonably necessary to:

* Operate the bot
* Comply with applicable law
* Respond to legitimate legal requests
* Protect the Discord server or its users

Discord may independently process information in accordance with its own policies.

## 7. Discord

Delicate operates through Discord and therefore interacts with Discord's systems.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Delicate is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Delicate is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Users should comply with Discord's age requirements and all applicable laws when using Discord.

## 9. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored moderation information.

However, no online service, computer system, or database can guarantee complete security.

Bot operators are responsible for protecting their bot credentials, database, hosting environment, and other sensitive configuration.

## 10. Your Questions and Requests

If you have questions about information stored by a particular Delicate deployment, contact the administrators of the Discord server where the bot is being used.

Server administrators may be able to provide information about moderation records associated with your account or explain their retention practices.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Delicate, its data practices, or the moderation system changes.

The **Last updated** date at the top of this document will be changed when significant updates are made.

## 12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or information stored by Delicate, please contact the administrators of the Discord server where Delicate is deployed.
