# 📦 Delicate — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Delicate Discord bot collects, why it is collected, and how it is used.

## 1. Information We Collect

The bot may collect and store the following information when you interact with it or when a moderation action is performed:

- Discord user ID
- Discord username
- Discord server ID
- Moderation action history
- Warning records
- Moderation reasons
- Moderation durations
- Case and warning numbers
- Date and time of moderation actions
- Discord user ID and username of the staff member who performed a moderation action

The bot does **not** intentionally collect passwords, payment information, private messages, or Discord account authentication credentials.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information collected by the bot is used only for purposes related to the administration and moderation of Clouddie's Cardboard Box.

This may include:

- Managing moderation actions
- Keeping warning history
- Applying automatic warning escalation
- Maintaining staff moderation logs
- Reviewing a member's moderation history
- Preventing abuse of the server
- Investigating moderation-related incidents

## 3. Where Information Is Stored

Moderation records are stored in the bot's database and may be retained for as long as they are reasonably necessary for server administration and moderation purposes.

The bot's database is not intended to be publicly accessible.

## 4. Who Can Access the Information

Moderation records are intended to be accessible only to authorized members of the Clouddie's Cardboard Box staff team.

Information may also be processed by Discord as part of providing the Discord platform and its services.

We do not intentionally make moderation records publicly available.

## 5. Data Retention

Moderation records may be retained for as long as they are useful for maintaining server safety, resolving moderation disputes, identifying repeated rule violations, and maintaining an accurate moderation history.

Server administration may remove or modify records when appropriate.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell, rent, or trade information collected by the bot.

Information collected by the bot is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the bot, comply with applicable law, respond to legitimate legal requests, or protect the server and its users.

## 7. Discord

The bot operates through Discord and therefore interacts with Discord's systems.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information beyond what is necessary for Discord server moderation.

Users should follow Discord's age requirements and applicable laws when using Discord.

## 9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored moderation information.

However, no online service or database can guarantee complete security.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot, its data practices, or the server's moderation system changes.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this document will be changed when significant updates are made.

## 11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the information stored by the bot, please contact the Clouddie's Cardboard Box server administration through the official Discord server.
